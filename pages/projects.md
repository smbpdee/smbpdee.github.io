## <i class="fa fa-book" aria-hidden="true"></i> SMB PDEE journal club forming

The Population Dynamics, Evolution and Ecology subgroup is forming an **online journal club**!

For each meeting, a leader will choose a recent, short (maximum roughly 12 pages) journal article of which they are not an author, at least four weeks before the meeting. The article should treat a timely topic that falls within the subgroup's purview. Participants will read the article before the meeting.

The club will meet via Zoom every four to eight weeks, depending on interest. At each meeting, the leader will moderate discussion of the article; a volunteer will serve as Zoom host.

If you're interested in the club, whether as leader, host or participant, please fill out the Google form at [link](https://forms.gle/vWrpjz7kQ9dadUNJ8). If accessing Google is difficult for you, please email Evan Curcio at [evan.curcio@gmail.com](mailto:evan.curcio@gmail.com).

We hope this club will provide a stimulating environment where subgroup members will enhance their understanding of the field while getting to know each other better.
